Why this exists

TOOLS TRACK TASKS, OR HOURS. RARELY BOTH.

Project
Gig
Task
Timesheet
Weekly reflection

A time register punches a hole when you arrive and another when you leave. You were counted. Everything in between was assumed.

Most software still works that way: a ticket opened, a ticket closed, hours logged, a number at the bottom. How the work actually went never makes it into the record.

Mulai ties every hour to the gig that spawned it, and every gig to the project it serves. When the chain is whole, the weekly report nearly writes itself, and planning stops being an argument about what happened. Then it asks the question the card never did: how was it?

Three ways to be here

TWO ROLES TODAY. ROOM FOR A THIRD.

Lead
Places and decides

Turns projects into gigs, one-off or recurring, plans the week and holds the line on what good looks like. Has the final word on every evaluation.

  • Gigs sized for a real week
  • Freeze or kill what is not working
  • Overdue work visible at a glance
Doer
Picks up and delivers

Works from a clear list of tasks, clocks in by dragging a gig onto the clock, and later says honestly how it went. That honesty becomes a record that is theirs.

  • Tasks and checklists, nothing extra
  • Time logged live or after the fact
  • Skills that grow in the matrix

How a gig moves

PLACE. DO. TRACK. REFLECT.

01
PLACE

A gig gets a project, a code, a Lead and a Doer. If it repeats, set it once and it spawns its own instances.

02
DO

The Doer works through the task list. The gig moves from matched to aligned, in progress and delivered.

03
TRACK

Drag a gig onto the clock. Place and time are noted without being asked. Forgot? Add the entry later.

04
REFLECT

Both people rate the work. The Lead's word is final. Weekly reports show what to repeat and what to stop.

What is built

FUNCTIONS INSTALLED

All of it is live and used every day, on real work.

Structure
PROJECTS, CATEGORIES, GIG CODES

Every gig sits in a project and carries a code that says what it is and where it belongs.

Lifecycle
FREEZE OR KILL, WITH A REASON

Pause a gig or a whole project without losing anything. Close one for good, with the reason on record.

Recurrence
WORK THAT REPEATS WITHOUT REPEATING

Weekly, fortnightly, monthly or on request. Instances and their checklists are created for you.

Tasks
ONE REGISTER FOR EVERY CHECKLIST

Tasks assigned to Lead or Doer, with stale and overdue work flagged across all gigs.

Time
DRAG TO CLOCK IN

Auto or manual entries, open timesheets, task check-off at clock-out and hours so far on each gig.

Reflection
HONEST RATINGS, EVERY TIME

Doer and Lead rate the same attributes. The Lead's average becomes the final score.

Planning
WEEK PLANNER AND PLAN VIEW

Drag gigs into next week's mornings and afternoons, then see who actually worked when.

Reports
WEEKLY REPORT AND DATA VIEW

Time, tasks and gigs for the week, plus three short written answers. Browse across people and weeks.

Skills
A SKILLS MATRIX THAT SHOWS GAPS

Rate yourself from Seed to Grove, see coverage across the team and where fewer than two people are strong.
