Gig Controls
One sheet for how a gig behaves. Pinned puts it first in your Timesheet (yours alone, nothing is hidden for good). Frozen pauses a gig and is fully reversible. Killed closes it for good with a reason on record. Neither touches logged time, tasks or evaluations.
